# Risk audit: `main...stage-1-mvp` (2026-10-19)

Auditor: `security-trading-risk-critic` agent. Verdict: **FIX BEFORE RELEASE**. No finding loses money
in read-only Stage 1, but H1/M1 could let the Go/No-Go drawdown check pass incorrectly and M2/M3 could
distort the paper-vs-backtest evidence.

Clean: secrets handling (SecretStr, redaction, no tracked secrets), Decimal discipline in money paths,
strict-cross fills with post-placement eligibility, stop exit at candle low, fair buy & hold, train-only
parameter selection, compose isolation (no ports, non-root, healthchecks, one-shot migrations).

| ID | Severity | Finding | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | HIGH | OOS max drawdown measured per window only; losses compounding across windows ignored | Chained OOS equity curve; criterion uses the worse of chained and per-window DD |
| M1 | MEDIUM | Drawdown sampled only at candle close (intra-bar wicks ignored) | Drawdown also evaluated at candle low |
| M2 | MEDIUM | Paper restart could duplicate fills/cycles and reuse client ids (snapshot once a minute, events immediately) | Snapshot after every fill batch; on resume prune events newer than the snapshot |
| M3 | MEDIUM | Gap backfill started at disconnect detection (up to 60 s late) and missed the trailing minute | Backfill from the session's last event; second backfill on the first tick after the gap |
| M4 | MEDIUM | A stopped session was silently reopened on restart | Stopped pairs stay stopped unless `pendulum paper run --rearm`; Telegram notice |
| L1 | LOW | Comparison backtest did not reuse min-step multiplier; no data-quality gate | Multiplier persisted with the session; >1% missing bars refused |
| L2 | LOW | One pair's `GridPlanError` crashed all sessions; resume built a throwaway plan | Per-pair error isolation; plan restored from state |
| L3 | LOW | Notifier could raise on non-JSON error bodies | JSON parsing guarded |
| L4 | LOW | Exit-policy timers not persisted | Timers in the trader snapshot |
| L5 | LOW | Paper healthcheck false negatives (quiet pairs, no running sessions) | Minute heartbeat; healthy when nothing is running |
| L6 | LOW | Digest buy & hold ignored fees/slippage | Same fee/slippage model as the backtest |
| L7 | LOW | `.env` world-readable | `chmod 600 .env` |
